SoundEtch is an audio and podcast recorder/editor that runs completely inside your browser.

Record, upload, edit, and transcribe with no setup and no subscription.

Your media stays yours.

Features

  • Record your microphone, browser, or computer audio instantly.
  • Invite guests to record with you.
  • Remove noise, rearrange and find your sound with robust editing tools.
  • Upload additional media. Dozens of supported formats.
  • Fast, accurate speech-to-text.
  • Text-based audio editing.
  • Automatic saving; accidentally closing the page won't destroy your progress.

SoundEtch stands on the shoulders of many open-source projects. We are grateful to their authors and communities:

Transcription

  • IBM Granite Speech 5.0 TurboCTC — speech-to-text model
  • Silero VAD — voice activity detection
  • ONNX Runtime Web — runs the models in the browser
  • fft.js — fast Fourier transform

Effects

  • AudioJS Dynamics — the De-esser, Limiter, and Gate effects are adapted from it (MIT licensed, by Dmitry Iv)
  • WEQ8 — parametric equalizer
  • DeepFilterNet — noise reduction

Media Import & Export

  • MediaBunny — media processing, muxing, and encoding
  • web-demuxer — demultiplexing of imported media files
  • LAME — MP3 encoding (WebAssembly bridge from the MediaBunny project)
  • libFLAC — FLAC encoding
  • libopus and libopusenc — Opus encoding
  • wasm-audio-resampler — sample-rate conversion, built on SoXR

Audio Engine & Connectivity

  • @descript/web-audio-js — offline audio rendering
  • ringbuf.js — real-time audio streaming between threads
  • PeerJS — peer-to-peer connectivity

Analysis

  • TF3RDL — spectrum analysis
