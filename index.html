About SoundEtch

SoundEtch is a browser-based audio recorder and editor built for quickly capturing ideas, interviews, and collaborative sessions without installing a desktop app.

You can record from microphones, tab/screen audio, or imported files, then trim, layer, and export in multiple formats.

Thanks for trying this preview. Feedback and rough edges are both welcome while the app keeps evolving.
