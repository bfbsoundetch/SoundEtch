AudioJS Dynamics
----------------

SoundEtch's De-esser, Limiter, and Gate effects are adapted from the following AudioJS Dynamics packages:

- https://github.com/audiojs/dynamics/tree/main/packages/dynamics-deesser
- https://github.com/audiojs/dynamics/tree/main/packages/dynamics-limiter
- https://github.com/audiojs/dynamics/tree/main/packages/dynamics-gate

The De-esser adaptation also incorporates the AudioJS envelope, compressor-gain, and biquad processing patterns used by dynamics-deesser.
